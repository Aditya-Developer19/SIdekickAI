# Top 3 Pizza Shops in New Delhi, India

Here are three of the finest pizza shops in New Delhi, India, renowned for their authentic crusts, premium ingredients, and delicious flavors:

---

## 1. Leo's Pizzeria
* **Style:** Authentic Neapolitan Wood-Fired Pizza
* **Address:** 28, Basant Lok Market, Vasant Vihar, New Delhi, Delhi 110057
* **Highlights:** 
  - Consistently ranked among the Top 50 Pizzerias in the Asia-Pacific region.
  - Features an authentic wood-fired oven and high-quality imported Italian ingredients.
  - Minimalist, warm, and dog-friendly ambiance.
* **Popular Dishes / Pizzas:**
  - Pepperoni Pizza Pie
  - Classic Margherita
  - Pancetta Pizza
  - Quattro Formaggi
  - Artisanal Tiramisu
* **Approx. Cost for Two:** ₹1,500 – ₹2,000
* **Timings:** 12:00 PM – 11:00 PM

---

## 2. EVOO Eatery & Pizzeria
* **Style:** Handcrafted Neapolitan & Artisanal Italian Cuisine
* **Address:** B-2, Ground Floor, Near Sri Aurobindo College, Shivalik Road, Malviya Nagar, New Delhi, Delhi 110017
* **Contact Phone:** +91 83684 28737
* **Highlights:**
  - Named after Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO), highlighting quality and fresh ingredients.
  - Slow-fermented dough creating light, airy wood-fired pizza crusts.
  - Highly rated for both pizzas and handmade pastas.
* **Popular Dishes / Pizzas:**
  - Spicy Italian Pizza
  - Smoked Chicken & Hot Honey Pizza
  - Classic Margherita
  - Spaghetti Carbonara
* **Approx. Cost for Two:** ₹1,200 – ₹1,800
* **Timings:** 12:00 PM – 11:00 PM
